About

I develop machine-learning and signal-processing methods to extract individual motor neuron activity from high-density electromyography (EMG). I apply this work to neural interfaces and assistive technology, including research after spinal cord injury.

Since 2025, I have been a Research Associate at the Imperial–Meta Wearable Neural Interfaces Research Centre at Imperial College London, working with Prof. Dario Farina. My current work connects EMG signal processing with wearable neural interfaces and their potential applications in neurorehabilitation.

During my PhD in AI and Machine Learning in the CDT in AI4Health at the Department of Computing, Imperial College London, I studied high-density electrode arrays and methods for connecting with the human spinal cord. I also build open-source tools for EMG decomposition and review.

Outside the lab

Making things

I grew up in Udine and joined the AGESCI Udine 1 Scout group when I was seven. One of my earliest memories there is earning the “Mani abili” (“Handicraft”) badge. Making things with my hands has always mattered to me; it was one of the main reasons I chose engineering. I still try to make time for it, whether I'm knitting, painting, or making pasta from scratch (the Italian in me). The tortellini here come in two versions: one crocheted, one made from dough.

Philosophy

My dad was a philosophy teacher, so philosophy was part of my life long before I studied it in high school. I've loved it ever since. During those years, I explored the work of Simone Weil, P. A. Florenskij, Hannah Arendt, Emmanuel Levinas, and F. M. Dostoevskij in depth.

When I think about philosophy, I picture Aristotle and his students discussing ideas as they walked. Some of my own best ideas come on a walk, and I hope to keep finding time to walk and think for as long as I can.

Outdoors

I've always enjoyed being outdoors and staying active. When I was little, climbing meant reaching the top of our bookcase and nearly giving my grandmother a heart attack; these days, I prefer hiking and bouldering. I love being on the water too - it reminds me of Trieste, a city I love. I joined the Imperial College Boat Club in my first year at Imperial. It was hard work, but I loved every minute of it.
